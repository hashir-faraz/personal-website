01Events

Public programming that brings people to the cultural house and records who came.

Painting Competition

  • Open call on a Qatari heritage theme, judged at the cultural house
  • Entry through a registration form — name, email, employer
  • Winning works stay on display in the gallery
  • One sponsorship slot available
ProducesA registered entrant list and one sponsor conversation.

Café Workshop Collaborations

  • History talks, bakhoor, calligraphy or sadu sessions hosted inside partner cafés
  • Ticketed through the café on an agreed split
  • Reaches audiences across Doha, not only Al Wakrah
  • Each café becomes a standing monthly venue
ProducesA ticketed attendee list per session, in a repeatable format.

Every event carries a registration step, so attendance becomes a contact list.

06Referral Partnerships

The cultural induction packaged at a wholesale rate, so recruitment and relocation firms can offer it under their own name.

How it appears
Cultural induction, included with every placement.Delivered in partnership with Embrace Doha
  • The partner sells the induction inside their own onboarding package
  • Embrace Doha appears on their site and in their client proposals
  • A fixed wholesale rate — no commission structure to negotiate
  • Bookings recur through the partner rather than one contract at a time
Target partners

08Corporate History Sessions

A guided session on the history of Qatar, delivered at the company's own offices.

The offer
  • Format — 60 to 90 minutes, on site, led by an Embrace Doha cultural expert
  • Audience — 20 to 100 staff, no venue needed on our side
  • Timing — National Day, Ramadan, and company learning weeks
  • Route to market — the two email segments from Avenue 03, with its own template
Why it opens doors
  • Removes travel to Al Wakrah, the most common reason a booking stalls
  • Lower commitment than a full induction, so an easier first yes
  • Fits a slot companies already run
  • The session itself is the sales meeting
ProducesA paid first engagement that converts into the full induction and venue bookings.

Priced below the full induction on purpose.
